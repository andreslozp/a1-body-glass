A1 TRUCK & RV CENTER - 5-MINUTE EXECUTIVE DEMO TALKING POINTS

1. THE BUSINESS GOAL (30 seconds)
This is not a social-media project. The goal is to create a repeatable B2B commercial pipeline for A1 Truck & RV Center: identify fleet companies, reach the people who control repair vendors, create conversations, and track those opportunities through meetings and repair work.

2. WHAT IS DIFFERENT (45 seconds)
A1 Truck & RV Center gets a separate commercial/fleet digital presence while still pointing to the main A1 corporate brand and domain. We do not need to change the existing A1 Body & Glass social media. The only technical help requested is the fleet subdomain and email aliases.

3. WHAT WILL EXIST IN FIVE DAYS (60 seconds)
- Fleet-focused landing page
- New LinkedIn company page
- New Instagram profile
- New Facebook page
- Google commercial campaign/tracking structure
- B2B email and phone outreach system
- Target-account and decision-maker list
- Commercial pipeline dashboard
- Initial B2B content
- Five-day performance report and 30-day operating plan

4. NUMBERS WE WILL SHOW (60 seconds)
Five-day operating targets:
- 40-60 target fleet accounts researched
- 30-45 decision-makers identified
- 35-50 personalized emails
- 25-40 call attempts
- 20-35 LinkedIn touches
Expected early-stage range:
- 5-10 responses or live connections
- 2-5 qualified fleet conversations
- 1-3 meeting/facility-tour targets
These are targets and ranges, not guaranteed sales results.

5. HOW IT WORKS (45 seconds)
Target company -> decision maker -> email/call/LinkedIn -> fleet conversation -> Carlos/A1 action -> meeting/inspection/estimate -> tracked outcome.

6. CLOSE (60 seconds)
At Day 5, the point is not to show a design project. The point is to show a working commercial acquisition engine: the digital presence is live, outreach is active, target accounts are in the pipeline, activity is measurable, and A1 can continue operating the campaign.
